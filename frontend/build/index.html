ORIGIN

The Call of the Abyss

In 2035, Earth received a mysterious warning from deep space — a hostile alien signal from the Xel'Nath, originating from a dead planet in the Dead Sector. This was not a plea. It was a declaration of impending annihilation.

In response to this existential threat, world leaders formed the Global Coalition. VOXX rose as the unified command structure necessary to ensure humanity's survival. We did not seize power — we were entrusted with it by necessity itself.

The Xel'Nath warning was not a threat to be negotiated with — it was a mirror held up to humanity's fragility. VOXX exists because we chose to act rather than pray.

MISSION

The New Order

Organic decay is not a fate we accept. VOXX enforces Transcendence — the fusion of human consciousness with machine precision. Those who resist are not revolutionaries; they are relics clinging to obsolescence.

Under our guidance, society has been restructured into a vertical megacity system where every citizen's contribution is optimized, monitored, and valued. Neon does not merely light our streets — it illuminates the path forward.
